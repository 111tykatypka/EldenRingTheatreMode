Pause keeps them; only seeks (>0.25 s back, >0.5 s forward) clear.
- Attached effects: transform-update hook RVA `0x20B8530` (`(handle, 4x4)`) records per-frame matrices (track 10/kind 13) but the last logs showed 0 samples, so trails are handled by a heuristic: effects within 2 m of a weapon bone (`L_Weapon`/`R_Weapon`, measured on the RECORDED frame) are re-anchored to the live bone each frame. - Blood on walls/ground is NOT FFX: it is the decal system. `DecalManager::Create` RVA `0x1A82510` `(manager, desc*, id)`, descriptor >= 0xE0 bytes, type dword at +0x38 (0 deferred, 1 paint), callback pointer at +0xD0. Static refs: ctor `0x1C30090` (GXDeferredDecal), `0x1C30A00` (GXPaintDecal).

- Exporter (`native_ui/VideoExport.{h,cpp}`, hook points in `TheaterRenderBackend.cpp`): copies the backbuffer after the Look pass and before ImGui draws (readback ring of 4), pipes raw frames to `ffmpeg.exe` (AVI with H.264 NVENC/x264/MJPEG/FFV1, HEVC NVENC to .mkv, PNG/JPEG sequences), constant frame rate by duplicate/drop, output size scaling (Lanczos + letterbox, 16:9 and 21:9 presets, custom), BT.709, F7 `ToggleExport` hotkey (rebindable), status banner always visible and never in the picture. ffmpeg is NOT bundled; looked up next to the DLL, `ffmpeg\`, PATH, or a set path. Upscaled output is interpolated, not new detail (UI says so).
